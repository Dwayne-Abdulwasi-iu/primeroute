Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Read how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: October 24, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you:

  • Create an account or register for our services
  • Place a shipping order or track a package
  • Contact us for customer support
  • Subscribe to our newsletter

This information may include:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Address information (pickup and delivery addresses)
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers)
  • Package details and tracking information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our shipping services
  • Process transactions and send related information
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and support messages
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except:

  • With your consent
  • To our trusted service providers who assist in operating our platform
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Secure data storage practices

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data
  • File a complaint with relevant authorities

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Provide personalized content

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@atlasdelivery.com
  • Phone: 1-800-ATLAS-1
  • Address: 123 Shipping Lane, Logistics City, LC 12345

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
