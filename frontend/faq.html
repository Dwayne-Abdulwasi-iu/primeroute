Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about PrimeRoute Cargos freight services, shipment tracking, and account support.

Tracking
Visit the Track Package section on our homepage and enter your tracking number. You'll get real-time status updates including estimated delivery time and current location. You can also receive SMS or email notifications by enabling alerts in your account settings.
Your tracking number is included in the confirmation email sent after your shipment is created. You can also find it in your account dashboard under "My Shipments".
Tracking updates occur at each scan point — pickup, sorting facility, transit hub, and delivery. There may be gaps between updates (up to 24 hours for international shipments). If there's no update for more than 48 hours, please contact our support team.
Shipping
Our freight options include air freight, ocean freight, and ground transport. Rates and transit times depend on the shipment and route. See our Shipping Guide for preparation details.
Use the Rate Calculator on our homepage. Enter your origin and destination ZIP codes, package weight, dimensions, and select a service type. The calculator will display an instant price estimate. Rates are based on dimensional weight, so accurate measurements are important.
We cannot ship hazardous materials, flammable liquids, illegal substances, firearms, explosives, live animals (outside authorized programs), cash, or unsupported perishables. For a full list, see our Shipping Guide.
Service availability depends on the route, cargo, and any applicable customs requirements. Contact our team to discuss a specific destination and shipment.
Account & Billing
Click "Login" in the top navigation bar and then select "Create Account". Fill in your name, email, and password. Once verified, you'll have access to shipment tracking, shipping history, saved addresses, and label printing.
Absolutely. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant third-party providers. We never store your full card details on our servers. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3.
Refunds are available for shipments that have not yet been picked up. Once a shipment is in transit, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. For late deliveries under our Overnight guarantee, a full refund of the shipping cost is issued automatically. Contact support to initiate a refund request.
Claims & Insurance
File a claim through your account dashboard or by contacting our support team within 30 days of the expected delivery date. For damaged packages, keep all original packaging and take clear photos as evidence. Claims are typically resolved within 5–10 business days.
Every shipment includes basic coverage up to $100 at no extra charge. Additional coverage is available for high-value items up to $10,000. Extended insurance can be added during checkout. See our Shipping Guide for more details.
