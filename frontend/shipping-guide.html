Shipping Guide

Helpful information about preparing freight, checking rates, and tracking shipments with PrimeRoute Cargos.

Last updated: October 24, 2025

1. Getting Started

Getting started with PrimeRoute Cargos is straightforward. Follow these steps to prepare a shipment:

  • Create a free account on our website
  • Enter your origin and destination addresses
  • Select a service type and calculate your rate
  • Pay securely and print your shipping label
  • Arrange a suitable collection or delivery handoff with our team

2. Service Options

Available transport options depend on the cargo and route. Contact us to confirm the right service for your shipment:

  • Air Freight — For time-sensitive and priority cargo
  • Ocean Freight — For larger consignments and international routes
  • Ground Transport — For regional and cross-border journeys
  • Cargo Handling — Storage and shipment coordination support

3. Packaging Requirements

Proper packaging protects your items and ensures timely delivery. Please follow these guidelines:

  • Use a sturdy, corrugated cardboard box
  • Wrap fragile items with at least 2 inches of cushioning material
  • Remove or cover old shipping labels from reused boxes
  • Seal all seams with pressure-sensitive plastic tape (at least 2 inches wide)
  • Do not over-pack — the box should close flat without bulging

4. Prohibited Items

The following items cannot be shipped through our services:

  • Hazardous materials, chemicals, or flammable liquids
  • Illegal drugs or controlled substances
  • Firearms, ammunition, or explosives
  • Live animals (except through authorized pet-shipping programs)
  • Perishable food items without proper refrigerated packaging
  • Cash, currency, or negotiable instruments

5. Weight & Size Limits

Packages must comply with the following limits for standard shipping:

  • Maximum weight: 150 lbs (68 kg)
  • Maximum length: 108 inches (274 cm)
  • Maximum girth (2×width + 2×height): 165 inches (419 cm)
  • Oversized packages may incur additional fees — contact us for a custom quote

6. Tracking Your Package

Every shipment comes with real-time tracking included at no extra cost:

  • Use the tracking number from your confirmation email
  • Visit our Track Package page and enter your tracking number
  • Receive SMS or email notifications at each delivery milestone
  • Track multiple packages at once from your account dashboard

7. Insurance & Claims

All packages include basic coverage. Additional insurance is available:

  • Basic coverage up to $100 is included free with every shipment
  • Extended coverage available for high-value items (up to $10,000)
  • File a claim within 30 days of the expected delivery date
  • Claims require proof of value and photos of any damage

8. International Shipping

For shipments crossing borders, additional requirements apply:

  • A completed customs declaration form is required for all international packages
  • Duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient unless agreed otherwise
  • Delivery times may vary due to customs clearance delays
  • Some countries have additional import restrictions — check before shipping

9. Need Help?

Our support team can assist with shipping questions:

  • Email: support@atlasdelivery.com
  • Phone: 1-800-ATLAS-1
  • Live Chat: Available on our website during business hours
  • Contact form — we respond within 2 business hours
